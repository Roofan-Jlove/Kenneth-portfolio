Why BrainBoost?

Studying Should Feel Like Playing

I believe students learn better when they enjoy the learning process. BrainBoost combines education with games, quizzes, and rewards to make studying less stressful, more enjoyable, and more motivating — no logins, no payments, no complicated backend, just one clean index.html.

LEVEL UP YOUR LEARNING 🎓

BrainBoost

A fun, gamified study companion built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No login, no payment, no complicated backend.

Non-Goals

  • No login system
  • No payment system
  • No complicated backend

Constraints

  • Works on mobile & desktop
  • Accessible text sizes
  • No exposed secrets
  • Single index.html file
