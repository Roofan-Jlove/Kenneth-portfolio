    Why we built this

    We got tired of to‑do apps that get in the way. So we built one that gets out of it.

    ToDoFlow started as a weekend frustration: every list app we tried made us sign up before we could type a single task, or buried "add" behind three taps. We wanted something as fast as a sticky note and as calm as a blank page — borrowing Apple's restraint and Linear's speed. No account, no sync delay, nothing to configure. Just a place to write down what matters and cross it off.
