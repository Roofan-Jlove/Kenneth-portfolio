01 Our Legacy

The Vision That
Sparked a Legacy

PaulMUN was founded in 2013 by patron Mr. Benny Vaz, built on early ideas from Anas Ahtesham and Alexander Robinson, to cultivate public speaking, diplomacy and leadership among St. Paul’s students. What began as a single conference has grown, edition by edition, into one of the school’s most enduring traditions.

PaulMUN I was led by founding Secretary-General Aaron Alexander — the first of many Paulians to take the podium and set the standard for every edition that followed.

An Ongoing
Legacy

Through twelve editions and a global pause in between, PaulMUN has kept growing — from PaulMUN I in 2013 to PaulMUN XI, “An Ongoing Legacy,” held in September. Each conference has produced confident speakers, critical thinkers and Pakistani MUN champions, carrying the spirit of diplomacy and excellence forward.

This year, PaulMUN XII convenes on 2–4 October 2026 — the next chapter in a legacy that started with a handful of delegates in a single school hall.

03

What Happens
at PaulMUN?

Delegates, Not Spectators

Student delegates each represent a country or role on a committee, researching real global issues and negotiating resolutions the way diplomats do at the actual United Nations.

Public Speaking Under Pressure

Formal debate, caucusing and crisis moments push every delegate to think on their feet — building the confident speakers and critical thinkers PaulMUN is known for producing.

A Pipeline to National MUN

Paulian delegates who cut their teeth at PaulMUN have gone on to represent Pakistan on the wider MUN circuit — carrying the school’s name well beyond Iqbal Shaheed Road.
